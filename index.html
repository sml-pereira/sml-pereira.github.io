Samuel Pereira

I am a music theorist and computational musicologist, curious about what musical structures reveal once they are translated into mathematics. I completed my PhD in Digital Media at the University of Porto in 2026 and now teach as an Invited Assistant Professor at the University of Coimbra, while also serving as Reviews Editor for the Revista Portuguesa de Musicologia.

I began as a composer, armed with a pencil, an eraser, and (I must confess!) a healthy disregard for deadlines. The pencil has since given way to Python; the deadlines remain equally aspirational.
